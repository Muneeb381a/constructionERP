Category setup reference

Five materials, five different units of sale

Sariya sells by weight but is bought by the piece. Cement is a bag until it isn't. Bricks are quoted per thousand. Sand depends on which river it came from. Girder gets weighed, not measured. This is what each one actually needs in the catalog.

⚠ Prices move weekly, sometimes daily — they track steel scrap, fuel, and the rupee. Everything below is a snapshot compiled late July 2026 from public Pakistani rate-tracking sites (sources at the bottom). Use it to set realistic starting prices and unit structures — re-check against a dealer before any large purchase order.

Bricks

Priced by the piece but always quoted per thousand — invoices and phone-quotes alike. Grade is a quality tier, not a brand.

TR Girder / T-Iron
